Dual-motor drive

All-wheel drive with instant torque and a smooth, quiet launch. Drive modes tune throttle, steering weight and regeneration.

Battery and charging

A 95 kWh pack with liquid cooling. Fast charging adds a long trip’s worth of range in about 20 minutes.

A wide central display, a digital driver cluster, wireless phone charging and over-the-air software updates.

Adaptive cruise control, lane keeping, blind-spot monitoring and automatic emergency braking come as standard.
